# 04 — Pillars

Four rules every design decision must respect. When in doubt, the pillar wins over
convenience, spectacle, or a cool idea.

## 1. Power has a price

Every shortcut to power costs sanity, corruption, or both. There is no clean build.

- A stronger rite costs more offerings and more corruption.
- A powerful artefact carries a drawback (an effect on sanity/corruption or a taboo).
- Reading to gain access costs sanity now and corruption forever.

**Test:** if a new feature makes the player stronger with no cost on an axis, it is wrong.

## 2. Knowledge is progression, not just gear

You advance by *learning* — translating, investigating, being taught — not only by
finding loot.

- Rites must be **learned** before they can be performed; the data existing is not enough.
- Cults and tomes are the two teaching channels.
- The codex records what you know; lore unlocks gate content.

**Test:** if a feature can be fully used without any learning step, it is probably gear,
not progression.

## 3. The horror is a presence, not a spawn

Dread comes from the world acting before the monster appears: weather, whispers,
corrupted terrain, cult activity, events.

- Ambient events telegraph bigger ones (whispers before a rift blooms).
- The Watcher follows; the Horror is felt before it is fought.
- Corruption is visible in the world, not only on a meter.

**Test:** if the horror's only expression is an entity that pathfinds toward you, it fails.

## 4. Factions remember

Cults track you, and helping one closes a door elsewhere.

- Reputation is per-player and per-cult.
- Opposed pairs apply cross-consequences.
- Services, dialogue and rites are reputation-gated.

**Test:** if faction choices are reversible with no lasting consequence, the pillar is unmet.

## Anti-pillars

Deliberately *not* goals:

- **No pure power fantasy.** No "become a god, ignore costs".
- **No jump-scare-only design.** Atmosphere over startle.
- **No content for content's sake.** Every mob/item/rite must serve a system.
- **No irreversible dead ends without warning.** Costs are severe but legible.

## How pillars map to systems

| Pillar | Primary systems |
|---|---|
| Power has a price | Sanity, Corruption, Ritual costs |
| Knowledge is progression | Codex, Cults, Tomes, Quests |
| Horror is a presence | Events, World, The Watcher, ambience |
| Factions remember | Cults, Reputation, Dialogue, Quests |

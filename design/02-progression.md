# 02 — Progression

How a player advances, from first whisper to endgame. Progression is **knowledge and
reputation** as much as numbers.

## The arc

| Stage | Player state | Unlocks | Pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Survive the whisper** | First tome/altar; sanity drains in the dark | Tier-1 rites, codex | Learning that reading costs you |
| **Find a faction** | Chose/attracted a cult; reputation > 0 | Services, tier-2 rites | Choosing sides |
| **Work the rites** | Reagents gathered; first rift opened | Summoning, corruption stage `Touched` | Corruption starts to matter |
| **Go deeper** | `Marked`+ corruption; skill tree depth | Tier-3 rites, dark paths, Veil | Resistance vs. summoning diverges |
| **The horror stirs** | High total corruption; rifts open | Endgame | Banish or summon |

## Experience & levels

- **XP sources:** completing quests, performing rites, defeating eldritch entities,
  discovering structures/lore, surviving events.
- **Curve:** rising cost per level; a soft cap around the `Marked` stage.
- **Points:** each level grants skill points (see `15-skills.md`).
- **No level gates rites directly** — corruption stage and *knowledge* do.

## What gates what

| Gate | Opens |
|---|---|
| Knowledge (learned rite) | Performing that rite |
| Cult rank (reputation) | Services, teaching, trade |
| Corruption stage | Dark rites, endgame, some dialogue |
| Skill nodes | Passive power, active abilities |
| Map discovery | Reagents, structures, rift access |
| Endgame path | The Horror encounter |

## Skill progression

Skill trees (`15-skills.md`) give passive power and a few active abilities. Trees:
investigation, occultism, corruption, divination, blood_magic, summoning. Capstones
change the rules (e.g. a corruption floor), not just numbers.

## Knowledge progression

- **Codex entries** unlock by reading, observing, or being taught.
- Some content is *invisible* until known (rituals, entities, reagents).
- **Tomes** grant knowledge at a sanity/corruption cost.
- **Cults** teach rites as a service at a rank threshold.

## Reputation progression

- Quest completion, offerings, and signature rites raise standing.
- Opposed cults apply cross-losses.
- Ranks unlock services (`+40` → Deep-Sworn, etc. — see `09-cults.md`).

## The endgame fork

Once corruption reaches `Claimed`, two paths open (`11-endgame.md`):

1. **Banish** — costly; resets much progression/corruption.
2. **Summon** — the Hollow Choir's goal; ends the run in their favour.
